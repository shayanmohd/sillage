First release.

Sillage is a diary for smells, a training programme for your nose, and a record of what is on your shelf.

Write a scent entry in forty seconds with a comparison anchor, and it comes back on the anniversary and in the same week next year. Thirty eight blind training sessions using your own kitchen. 352 lexicon cards, and a vocabulary that grows as you use them.

No account, no cloud, no ads, and no internet permission at all.
